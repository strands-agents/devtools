# Goals: diagrams

- Repo diagram instructions were looked for; when they exist, they were followed over this skill's
  defaults.
- No hand-rolled browser setup (apt, .debs, `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`, fontconfig) unless the preinstalled
  Chromium actually failed, with the error shown.
- Each changed diagram was rendered, in both themes where the destination has two, and the PNGs were
  actually opened with `read`, not just listed.
- Labels are legible in the screenshots; nothing clipped, overlapping, or blank.
- The report names the diagrams, the PNG paths, and anything that couldn't be
  rendered or viewed.
